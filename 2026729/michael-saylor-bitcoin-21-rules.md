# Michael Saylor's 21 Rules of Bitcoin

A summary of key insights from Michael Saylor's Bitcoin Keynote.

---

## The Singularity of Bitcoin

Bitcoin represents a **singularity** where science collides with economics. Saylor draws parallels to historical moments when scientific advances revolutionized fields like astronomy and medicine. 

According to Saylor, bitcoin is a similar paradigm shift in economics—providing **perfect money** in a world that has long relied on flawed monetary systems (seashells, tobacco, paper currencies). This collision ushers in a new age of economic insight, profoundly impacting:

- Political systems
- Financial structures  
- Global perspectives

---

## The 21 Rules

### Part 1: Understanding and Embracing Bitcoin

#### Rule 1: Those Who Understand Bitcoin Buy It
> Those who truly understand bitcoin will inevitably purchase it; those who do not will criticize it.

Once the paradigm shift clicks, the urge to acquire bitcoin becomes overwhelming. Critics simply haven't grasped its transformative potential.

#### Rule 2: Everyone Is Against Bitcoin Before They Support It
Opposition to bitcoin is expected from bankers, politicians, and the public. This resistance is a sign of bitcoin's revolutionary nature—not a cause for discouragement.

#### Rule 3: The Ongoing Journey of Understanding Bitcoin
Understanding bitcoin is an ongoing process. True believers recognize their learning is never complete. Bitcoin is more than a protocol or software—it's an economic, monetary, and ideological force that will continue to evolve.

---

### Part 2: Bitcoin as Perfect Money

#### Rule 4: Bitcoin is Powered by Chaos
Unlike fiat currencies, stocks, or real estate (which are susceptible to government and corporate failures), bitcoin **benefits from entropy**. 

*Metaphor:* Like a Swiss bank during World War II, bitcoin gains value as chaos increases.

#### Rule 5: Bitcoin as the Ultimate Store of Value
> Bitcoin is the only game in the casino where everyone can win.

It's a bet on chaos outperforming other investments. The only way to lose:
- Not playing
- Quitting too soon

Over a long enough time horizon, bitcoin will outperform all other investments due to its resilience against chaos.

#### Rule 6: The Importance of Total Commitment
Partial investments or lack of control over holdings leave investors vulnerable. To truly benefit, one must be fully committed—both in investment and security.

#### Rule 7: Bitcoin Redefines Ownership
Bitcoin is the **first asset in human history** that one can truly own, independent of external powers. While other property is subject to confiscation, regulation, or decay, bitcoin's value only grows over time, and no one can take it away.

---

### Part 3: Overcoming Traditional Economic Models

#### Rule 8: Everyone Gets Bitcoin at the Price They Deserve
Everyone buys bitcoin at the price they deserve based on their understanding and timing. Early adopters are rewarded; latecomers and skeptics pay higher prices.

#### Rule 9: Bitcoin Escapes the Matrix
The traditional financial system is a matrix of synthetic metrics and false indicators. Bitcoin offers an escape—a way to preserve and grow wealth outside conventional, flawed economic models.

#### Rule 10: Insight Is Restricted to Those with a Need to Know
Not everyone understands bitcoin because not everyone needs to. Those comfortable within the current system have no immediate need. Those facing economic uncertainty or disruption will see its true value.

#### Rule 11: All Models Will Be Destroyed
Bitcoin challenges and ultimately destroys traditional economic models—like ancient military strategies becoming obsolete in the face of modern warfare. Bitcoin makes many existing models irrelevant.

---

### Part 4: The Cure for Economic Ills

#### Rule 12: The Orange Pill
Bitcoin is the cure for toxic economic systems of the past. The **"orange pill"** signifies bitcoin's transformative power in detoxifying capital and currency systems.

#### Rule 13: Be for Bitcoin, Not Against Fiat
Rather than opposing fiat currencies, promote bitcoin. Being *for* bitcoin and recognizing its superior qualities is more productive than merely criticizing fiat systems.

#### Rule 14: Bitcoin is for Everyone
Bitcoin benefits everyone—even those who initially oppose or don't understand it. Enemies and competitors adopting bitcoin ultimately strengthen the network and increase its value for all holders.

#### Rule 15: Learn to Think in Bitcoin
Adopt a bitcoin-centric mindset:
- Evaluate investments through the lens of bitcoin's potential
- Focus on long-term returns and stability
- Move beyond traditional financial metrics

---

### Part 5: Respecting Bitcoin

#### Rule 16: You Don't Change Bitcoin
Bitcoin is a powerful, decentralized force that individuals cannot change. People should adapt to bitcoin, not the other way around.

*Comparison:* Those who try to change bitcoin are like intellectuals attempting to teach eagles how to fly.

#### Rule 17: Laser Eyes and Focus
The **laser eyes meme** symbolizes unwavering focus on bitcoin. Avoid distractions and overconfidence. Maintain focus on bitcoin's long-term potential.

#### Rule 18: Respect Bitcoin or Be Humbled
Respect for bitcoin is crucial. Those who underestimate its power risk being humbled by its success and resilience.

---

### Part 6: Never Sell Your Bitcoin

#### Rule 19: Bitcoin is Life
Bitcoin equals life energy—essential for preserving and growing wealth. Never sell your bitcoin. It's like keeping a fire burning to ensure survival.

#### Rule 20: Do Not Diversify
> Diversification means selling a winning asset to buy lesser assets.

Only sell bitcoin in dire circumstances. Otherwise, maintain holdings to benefit from long-term growth.

---

### Part 7: Spreading Bitcoin

#### Rule 21: Spread Bitcoin with Love, Not Hate
Spread bitcoin through positive engagement, not hostility. Educate and convert skeptics with kindness, making adoption smoother and more widespread.
