## What is a Bitcoin Halving?

A **halving** is a programmed event that reduces the Bitcoin mining reward by 50%. It occurs every 210,000 blocks (approximately every 4 years) and is designed to control inflation by gradually reducing the rate of new Bitcoin creation.

**Key principle:** Less new supply + steady/growing demand = upward price pressure

## Halving Cycle Pattern

### The Typical 4-Year Cycle

```
Year 1 (Halving Year):     Accumulation → Price consolidation
Year 2 (Post-Halving):     Bull Market → New all-time highs
Year 3 (Peak/Correction):  Distribution → Major correction begins
Year 4 (Bear/Recovery):    Bear Market → Bottoming, accumulation resumes
```

| Cycle | Halving to Peak | Time to Peak | Drawdown After Peak |

| Cycle 3 | $8,600 → $69,000 | ~18 months | -77% |

## Why Halvings Matter

### 1. Supply Shock
Each halving reduces the flow of new Bitcoin by 50%, creating a supply shock when demand remains constant or grows.

### 2. Miner Economics
Miners must sell BTC to cover costs. When rewards halve, selling pressure from miners drops significantly.

```
Pre-2024 Halving:  900 BTC/day × $60,000 = $54M daily sell pressure
Post-2024 Halving: 450 BTC/day × $60,000 = $27M daily sell pressure
                                           ─────────────────────────
                   Reduction:              $27M less selling per day
```

### 3. Scarcity Narrative
Halvings reinforce Bitcoin's scarcity story, often generating media attention and renewed investor interest.

### 4. Stock-to-Flow
The halving increases Bitcoin's stock-to-flow ratio, a metric comparing existing supply to new production.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Every halving has preceded a major bull market** — though past performance doesn't guarantee future results

2. **Returns diminish each cycle** — 95x → 30x → 8x → ? (as market cap grows, percentage gains naturally decrease)

3. **Time to peak extends** — 12 months → 17 months → 18 months

4. **Supply is becoming scarcer** — Only ~1.3 million BTC left to mine over the next ~116 years

5. **Institutional adoption accelerates** — ETFs, corporate treasuries, and nation-states are now participants
