## The Path to Millionaire Status: Saylor's "Maxi" Framework

From Bitcoin 2024, Saylor outlined three levels of Bitcoin commitment:

### Level 1: Bitcoin Maxi
- Believe in Bitcoin as the best investment
- Allocate a significant portion to BTC
- **Result:** Path to becoming a millionaire

### Level 2: Double Maxi
- Move ALL other financial assets into Bitcoin
- Funnel as much earnings as possible into BTC
- **Result:** Accelerated wealth building

### Level 3: Triple Maxi
- All assets in Bitcoin
- All earnings into Bitcoin
- **Take on debt to buy more Bitcoin** (leverage)
- **Result:** Maximum wealth potential (but highest risk)

> "Leverage is the key to amplifying your Bitcoin returns." — Saylor

## Why Millionaires Will Be Created (Saylor's Thesis)

### 1. Supply Shock
- Only 21 million Bitcoin will ever exist
- Each halving reduces new supply by 50%
- Lost coins (~3-4 million) reduce circulating supply further

### 2. Demand Explosion
- Spot ETFs bringing institutional money
- Corporate treasuries adopting Bitcoin
- Nation-states creating Bitcoin reserves
- Retail FOMO as prices rise

### 3. The Math
```
Limited Supply + Increasing Demand = Higher Prices

$1 trillion market cap → $50K per BTC (2024)
$21 trillion market cap → $1M per BTC (2030s)
$280 trillion market cap → $13M per BTC (2045)
```

## Saylor's Warning

While bullish, Saylor acknowledges:

> "Bitcoin's long-term returns will eventually trend lower over time."

- Early adopters see the highest returns
- As Bitcoin matures, growth rate slows
- 29% annual returns won't last forever
- **But:** Even lower returns beat traditional assets
